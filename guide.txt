-- GAMEPLAY --
Gamepad controls are only avaliable for 2 player mode.
One player uses keybaord and the other uses the contoller

W/d-pad up/xbx A/nin B - jump
A/d-pad left/left stick - move left
D/d-pad right/left stick - move right
space/xbx LB/nin L - block
left shift/xbx RT/nin ZR - dash
J/xbx Y/nin X - spin kick
K/xbx X/nin Y - punch
L/xbx B/nin A - side kick
I/xbx RB/nin R - throw fireball

-- MENU --
escape/xbx menu/nin '+' - open/close menu
xbx A/nin A - click button

-- DEBUG -- 
H - show collision boxes
